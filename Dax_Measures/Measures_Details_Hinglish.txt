=============================================================================
CATEGORY 1: OVERALL DATASET STATS
=============================================================================


1. Total Songs

Purpose: Current filter context me total chart entries (rows) ki ginti.
Note: yeh chart-entry days count karta hai, distinct songs nahi — jo song
50 din chart me raha, woh is count me 50 baar contribute karega.

Syntax:
Total Songs = COUNTROWS(fact_chart_entries)


2. Distinct Songs

Purpose: Sirf distinct song TITLES ki count, artist ko ignore karke. Do
alag artists ke ek jaise-titled songs ko bhi ek hi count karta hai.

Syntax:
Distinct Songs = DISTINCTCOUNT(fact_chart_entries[song])


3. Distinct Artists

Purpose: Current filter context me present unique artists ki count.

Syntax:
Distinct Artists = DISTINCTCOUNT(fact_chart_entries[artist])


4. Avg Popularity

Purpose: Current filter context ki chart entries ka average Spotify
popularity score.

Syntax:
Avg Popularity = AVERAGE(fact_chart_entries[popularity])


5. Max Popularity

Purpose: Current filter context me present sabse highest popularity score.

Syntax:
Max Popularity = MAX(fact_chart_entries[popularity])


6. Min Popularity

Purpose: Current filter context me present sabse lowest popularity score.

Syntax:
Min Popularity = MIN(fact_chart_entries[popularity])

CATEGORY 2: DURATION

7. Avg Duration Minutes

Purpose: Average song length, milliseconds se minutes me convert kiya
gaya. Yeh ek song-level attribute hai (dim_songs par rehta hai), isliye
yeh jo bhi songs context me hain unhe reflect karta hai — chart-day counts
ko nahi.

Syntax:
Avg Duration Minutes = AVERAGE(dim_songs[duration_ms]) / 60000


8. Max Duration Minutes

Purpose: Current filter context me present sabse lambi song duration,
minutes me.

Syntax:
Max Duration Minutes = MAX(dim_songs[duration_ms]) / 60000


9. Min Duration Minutes

Purpose: Current filter context me present sabse chhoti song duration,
minutes me.

Syntax:
Min Duration Minutes = MIN(dim_songs[duration_ms]) / 60000

CATEGORY 3: EXPLICIT CONTENT
=============================================================================


10. Explicit Songs

Purpose: Un chart entries ki count jinke underlying song explicit tagged
hain. dim_songs -> fact_chart_entries relationship ke through filter
karta hai.

Syntax:
Explicit Songs = CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(fact_chart_entries), dim_songs[is_explicit] = TRUE())


11. Non-Explicit Songs

Purpose: Un chart entries ki count jinke underlying song explicit tagged
nahi hain.

Syntax:
Non-Explicit Songs = CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(fact_chart_entries), dim_songs[is_explicit] = FALSE())


12. Pct Explicit Songs

Purpose: Current context ki saari chart entries me se explicit-tagged
entries ka share — 0 aur 1 ke beech ek proportion ki tarah.

Syntax:
Pct Explicit Songs = DIVIDE([Explicit Songs], [Total Songs], 0)


13. Avg Popularity Explicit

Purpose: Sirf explicit-tagged chart entries ka average popularity score.

Syntax:
Avg Popularity Explicit = CALCULATE(AVERAGE(fact_chart_entries[popularity]), dim_songs[is_explicit] = TRUE())


14. Avg Popularity NonExplicit

Purpose: Sirf non-explicit chart entries ka average popularity score.

Syntax:
Avg Popularity NonExplicit = CALCULATE(AVERAGE(fact_chart_entries[popularity]), dim_songs[is_explicit] = FALSE())

20. Singles Count

21. Albums Count

Purpose: Full album ka part hokar release hue songs se belong karne wali

Syntax:
Albums Count = CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(fact_chart_entries), dim_songs[album_type] = "album")


22. Pct Singles

23. Songs per Artist

25. Avg Popularity per Artist

Purpose: Ek given artist ki chart entries ka average popularity score.

Avg Popularity per Artist = AVERAGE(fact_chart_entries[popularity])


26. Position1 Hits per Artist

Purpose: Ek given artist ke #1 chart-entry days ki count. [Position 1

Position1 Hits per Artist = CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(fact_chart_entries), fact_chart_entries[position] = 1)


27. Artist Rank by Chart Entries

Purpose: Total chart entries ke hisaab se har artist ka numeric rank,
highest pehle. RANKX demonstrate karta hai — har artist par iterate karke,
har ek ka [Songs per Artist] value doosron se compare karta hai.

Syntax:
Artist Rank by Chart Entries = RANKX(ALL(dim_songs[artist]), [Songs per Artist], , DESC)


28. Top 10 Artist Entries

Purpose: Sirf top 10 sabse zyada chart karne wale artists ki combined
chart-entry count. TOPN demonstrate karta hai — aggregate karne se pehle
table ka top-ranked subset select karta hai.

Syntax:
Top 10 Artist Entries = CALCULATE([Total Songs], TOPN(10, ALL(dim_songs[artist]), [Songs per Artist], DESC))


29. Pct of All Entries by Artist

Purpose: Kisi bhi doosre active filter ki parwaah kiye bina, ek given
artist ka har kabhi record hui chart entry me share. ALL() ko poore-table
level par demonstrate karta hai, dim_songs ke sirf ek column ke bajaye
saare filters ek saath hata deta hai.

Syntax:
Pct of All Entries by Artist = DIVIDE([Songs per Artist], CALCULATE([Total Songs], ALL(dim_songs)))

35. Weekend Entries Count

Purpose: Saturday ya Sunday ko aane wali chart entries. FILTER()
demonstrate karta hai, dim_dates ko row-by-row ek condition ke against
evaluate karte hue. weekday_num convention: Monday=1 se Sunday=7 tak, toh
weekend = {6, 7}.

Syntax:
Weekend Entries Count = CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(fact_chart_entries), FILTER(dim_dates, dim_dates[weekday_num] IN {6, 7}))

36. Days Charted

Purpose: Ek given song ne total kitne dinon tak chart par kahin bhi
appear kiya — zaroori nahi ki consecutively. [Total Songs] se identical
formula hai; clarity ke liye alag naam.

Syntax:
Days Charted = COUNTROWS(fact_chart_entries)


37. Days to No1

Purpose: Ek song ke release date aur usne pehli baar position 1 hit karne
ki date ke beech ka gap, days me. Jo songs kabhi #1 nahi bane, unke liye
BLANK() return karta hai, ek misleading zero ya negative value ke bajaye.

Syntax:
Days to No1 =
    VAR ReleaseDate = MIN(dim_songs[release_date_cleaned])
    VAR First1Date = CALCULATE(MIN(fact_chart_entries[date]), fact_chart_entries[position] = 1)
    RETURN IF(ISBLANK(First1Date), BLANK(), DATEDIFF(ReleaseDate, First1Date, DAY))

44. Position Range

Purpose: Ek given song ke poore charting run me, uske best aur worst
chart position ke beech ka spread.

Syntax:
Position Range = MAX(fact_chart_entries[position]) - MIN(fact_chart_entries[position])


45. Position StdDev

Purpose: Ek given song ki daily chart position ka standard deviation — uska
run kitna volatile tha, sirf best-to-worst range se aage. STDEV.P
demonstrate karta hai: population standard deviation, jo yahan appropriate
hai kyunki humare paas har song ke chart-days ka poora set hai, sample
nahi.

Syntax:
Position StdDev = STDEV.P(fact_chart_entries[position])
